Meet RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It lets a language model answer from your documents, not just its memory.

First, split your documents into small chunks. Turn each chunk into an embedding, a list of numbers that captures meaning. Store them in a vector database.

When a user asks a question, embed the question too. Search the database for the closest chunks.

Finally, give the question plus the retrieved chunks to the model. It writes an answer grounded in your data. An answer you can trust.
